## Title

> [Resource] I built a free toolkit for independent language tutors — country-aware rates, contracts, lesson planning, no signup or platform fee

## Body

I tutor part-time and got tired of three things: (1) stitching seven free templates together for what should be one workflow, (2) handing 30% of my rate to a platform that adds little once a parent is booked, and (3) ChatGPT prompts that don't know what country my student is in or what CEFR level B1 actually means in practice.

So I built [Slatework](https://slatework.tools) — free, no signup, ten tools, country-aware for 7 markets (US, UK, CA, AU, NZ, IE, HK).

What's in it:

- **Hourly rate calculator** — country + language pair + experience → suggested low/median/high. Shows what you'd net on italki / Preply / Cambly / Verbling vs. private side-by-side.
- **Tax & self-employment guide** — country-specific. UK trading allowance, US 1099 thresholds, HK Business Registration, etc.
- **Setup walkthrough** — what you actually need to start (registration, insurance, safeguarding for minors).
- **Parent-tutor contract builder** — fills in your name/rate/policy and prints a clean PDF, all in your browser. Nothing sent to my server.
- **Lesson plan generator** — takes target language, level, mode (1:1 / small group / classroom), and goal; returns a time-blocked plan with exit ticket and differentiation notes.
- **Worksheet + answer key generator** — gap-fill / multiple-choice / short-answer / reading comprehension.
- **CEFR proficiency mapper** — Can-Do statements (rule-based, runs in browser) OR optional AI mode for a writing sample.
- **Marking accelerator** — paste a student's writing, get categorised errors + three feedback variants (warm / direct / rubric-mapped). Accepts photos of handwritten work.
- **Payment methods reference** — per-country domestic + international (FPS, PayMe, Wise, Zelle, Interac etc.).

What I want from this post:

1. **Tutors in countries I haven't covered yet** — tell me which one, I'll add the JSON country pack. India, Singapore, Philippines, EU markets are next on the queue.
2. **Anything that's wrong** — wrong tax form name, dead link, weird default. The country packs are JSON, easy to fix.
3. **What's missing** — what tool would actually save you 30 minutes a week?

Privacy posture: nothing about your students leaves your browser unless you're using the AI tools, which send to Anthropic and aren't logged or stored. The contract builder is fully client-side. Newsletter (optional) is SHA-256-hashed, no plaintext email storage. There's a `/privacy` page that's specific, not theatre.

Caveat: I'm a tutor who can code, not the other way around. Some of the rate ranges are calibrated against what I see in my own market and the platforms' published tutor counts. If your country's numbers look off, please tell me.

Built solo, free, and intends to stay free for the 10 tools. Affiliate links to tools you'd already pick (Wise, Stripe Link) cover hosting; an optional Pro tier may come later for power users with bigger AI quotas.

Happy to answer questions in the comments.
