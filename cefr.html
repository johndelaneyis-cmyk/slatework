// CEFR · PLACEMENT

CEFR proficiency mapper.

Place a student at A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, or C2 using the standard CEFR Can-Do statements. Pick the mode that matches the moment.

Runs entirely in your browser. We don't see anything you enter.

Answer for each statement: Yes, Partial, or No.

Don't include the student's name or identifying info. The writing sample is sent to Anthropic for processing and is not stored by us.
Pick the language being taught. Two English options: native/advanced vs. ESL/EFL.

How photo upload works: when you attach an image, we extract the text via Google Cloud Vision OCR (handwriting-trained) and drop it into the box below. Review and edit the extracted text — OCR isn't perfect, especially cursive — then click Assess. The placement model only ever sees text, not your photo.
